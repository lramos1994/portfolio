Section 2

SolarSys

SolarSys is a TypeScript and Vite app that generates animated solar systems as self-contained SVG files, entirely in the browser, with no backend. The generator is deterministic, so the same seed always produces the same universe, and that is what makes it testable: Vitest covers the generator, Playwright covers the animation.
